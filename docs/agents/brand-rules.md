# Brand rules

The rules from the ESN Visual Identity Manual that apply to this site. Check any change to logos, colours, images or copy against them. Page numbers refer to the 2023 edition of the manual.

1. **One ESN star per view.** Show at most one ESN star or logo in any one view, apart from the ESN Prague United logo in the site header. A Section logo appears only on that Section's own page, never where several Sections are listed together. Partner logos are exempt (p.18). The favicon is browser chrome, not part of a view, so it is exempt too.
2. **Show logos exactly as provided.** No cropping, stretching, recolouring, effects, outlines or rotation. Fit logos to their box at their natural shape; never crop them to a hotspot (pp.8, 15–16).
3. **Legible background and a safe zone.** Every logo needs a background it's legible on and a clear safe zone around it. No full-colour logo on a brand-colour fill or on a busy photo (pp.13, 17).
4. **The ESN star is never decoration.** Don't use it for bullets, dividers, patterns or ornaments (p.17).
5. **Only the 5 ESN colours.** Use only the 5 ESN colours, at their exact values, as brand colours (p.9).
6. **British English.** Write site copy in British English: colour, programme, organise (p.11).

The manual also forbids photos showing drunkenness, and photos with alcohol as the main subject (pp.27, 29). The Studio reminds editors of this on every photo field.
